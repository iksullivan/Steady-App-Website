How it works

1

Add your medications

Name, dose and the times you take them. Things like pain relief can be logged only when you need them.

2

Get reminded. Again.

At each scheduled time you get a notification. Ignore it and another arrives every 15 minutes. You choose the interval in Settings.

3

Tap Taken

The reminders stop and the dose goes into your history, so you can see at a glance which days were complete.

Questions

What is the TestFlight beta?

Steady isn't on the App Store yet. TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying pre-release apps. Beta builds can have bugs and may change before launch, so keep another reminder in place for anything critical while you test.

Will it really keep reminding me?

Yes. By default a missed dose comes back every 15 minutes until you tap Taken. You can change that to every 30 minutes, every hour or every 2 hours — for all your medications or just one — and you can snooze a single reminder.

What if I took it but forgot to tap Taken?

Tap Taken whenever you notice. Missed an entire dose? Open History and mark it taken, and the record shows when you did.

Does it work when the app is closed, or offline?

Yes. Reminders are scheduled on your device ahead of time, so they arrive without Steady open and without a connection.

Will Focus or Do Not Disturb block it?

A Focus can hold reminders back. To be safe, allow Steady in each Focus you use.

Can I share my history with my doctor?

You can export your history from Settings and send it however you like. Nothing is shared automatically.

Is Steady medical advice?

No. It's a reminder tool. Don't rely on it as your only safeguard, and talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your medications.
